Sheet: checking proof-of-delivery photos
Is there a parcel at the door, and where?
Each lesson runs Puzzle → Explore → Name → Challenge (interactive-educator method), for a learner who knows the chain rule and linear algebra but has no picture of networks yet. It follows the Regularization Lab. Source deck: M. Soleymani, "Convolutional Neural Networks" (after Stanford cs231n and CMU 11-785), Sharif University of Technology. The doorstep photos are made-up 10–12 pixel images, the colours are invented, and every filter is hand-set rather than trained, so you can see exactly what each weight does. Weight counts in lessons 5 and 6 are exact for the layer shapes shown.
