Optimization Lab II

Deep Learning · Training Neural Networks: Optimization II · six hands-on lessons
Sheet: fitting delivery fees from the order log
Maps: loss over rate & fee · brighter = lower loss · + = best fit
Each lesson runs Puzzle → Explore → Name → Challenge (interactive-educator method), for a learner who knows the chain rule and linear algebra but has no picture of networks yet. It follows the Optimization Lab for part I. Source deck: M. Soleymani, "Training Neural Networks: Optimization" (Optimization II), Sharif University of Technology (after CMU 11-785 and Stanford cs231n). Orders, fees and the networks in lessons 4–5 are made-up teaching models: lessons 3 and 5 plot the exact expected SGD loss, the others run real SGD with a fixed random seed.
