Each lesson runs Puzzle → Explore → Name → Challenge (interactive-educator method), for a learner who knows the chain rule and linear algebra but has no picture of networks yet. It follows the Training Lab. Source deck: M. Soleymani, "Training Neural Networks: Regularization" (after CMU 11-785 and Stanford cs231n), Sharif University of Technology. Orders, ETAs, traffic feeds and networks are made-up teaching data with fixed random seeds. The "network" in lessons 1, 2 and 5–7 is a degree-9 polynomial (10 weights) so every fit is exact and instant; lessons 3 and 4 use a linear unit and a 6-unit ReLU layer, whose dropout averages are computed exactly rather than sampled.
