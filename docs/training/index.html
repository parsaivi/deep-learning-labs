Training Lab

Deep Learning · Training Neural Networks: Practical Issues & Batch Normalization · ten hands-on lessons
Sheet: the delivery-time (ETA) network
Dots = orders · ◆ = the order you follow · ▲ = outside the healthy band
Each lesson runs Puzzle → Explore → Name → Challenge (interactive-educator method), for a learner who knows the chain rule and linear algebra but has no picture of networks yet. It follows the Optimization Labs. Source decks: M. Soleymani, "Training Neural Networks: Practical Issues" (after Stanford cs231n) and "Training Neural Networks: Batch Normalization" (after CMU 11-785 and cs231n), Sharif University of Technology. Orders, ETAs and the networks are made-up teaching models with fixed random seeds: lessons 3, 4 and 10 run real forward passes or gradient descent; the accuracy map of lesson 5 and the training drift of lesson 9 are hand-made curves.
